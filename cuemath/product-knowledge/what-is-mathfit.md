## 1. MathFit Framework

MathFit is Cuemath's core mission and brand framework — not just a feature, but the philosophy behind everything they build.

**Definition:** Children become MathFit when learning builds *proficiency and mindset together.*

### FUAR — Four Dimensions of Mathematical Proficiency

| Pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **F — Fluency** | Speed and accuracy |
| **U — Understanding** | Conceptual depth |
| **A — Application** | Real-world math |
| **R — Reasoning** | Deep mathematical thinking and logical reasoning |

### GRIC — Mindset Dimensions (used in MathFit Assessments)

Growth mindset, Reasoning, Interest, Confidence

### Key Message for Blogs

> "In an AI-first world, answers are instant — but clear thinking isn't. MathFit is mathematical fitness: helping kids frame problems, reason through ambiguity, spot patterns, explain their ideas, and become confident problem solvers."

### MathFit in the Classroom

- MathFit is delivered through 1:1 sessions, MathGym Daily Workouts, Curriculum V3.1, and MathFit Assessments
- The MathFit promise is made real in the live class — when tutor and student sit together
- CEO Manan Khurma teaches every day to personally understand and uphold the MathFit promise

| Skill | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Fluency** | Knowing facts, formulas, vocabulary; executing procedures accurately and efficiently | Knows 7×8=56; recalls area of triangle formula |
| **Understanding** | The "why" behind the "what" — conceptual grasp | Knows 3×4=12 means "3 groups of 4"; can show it with diagrams |
| **Application** | Tackles word problems and real-world situations; chooses right tools, checks if answer makes sense | Uses equations, graphs, tables, diagrams |
| **Reasoning** | Spots patterns, makes logical arguments, justifies thinking, approaches novel problems | Can reason to a solution even without prior exposure to problem type |

**How they connect:** Understanding develops fluency. Reasoning identifies which procedures to apply. Application requires all three working together.

### Pillar 2 — Mathematical Mindset (GRIC)

Studies show students' attitudes toward math significantly impact achievement.

| Attribute | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Interest** | Genuine enjoyment of mathematical exploration; intrinsic motivation; curiosity about patterns, puzzles, problems |
| **Confidence** | Believes "I will be successful"; approaches new problems assuming they can figure it out; confidence ≠ no struggle — it means believing struggle leads to achievement |
| **Growth Mindset** | Believes ability develops through effort, good strategies, and guidance; sees mistakes as learning opportunities; tries first, seeks help if needed, never gives up |
| **Relevance** | Sees math as a tool for navigating the world — splitting bills, understanding news statistics, planning savings |

#### Cuemath Features → Mathematical Mindset

| Mindset Attribute | Cuemath Features |
|---|---|
| Interest | Interactive simulations; energisers (brain teasers, mazes, riddles); puzzles; stickers for collaboration |
| Confidence | Gradual exposure to challenging tasks; stickers to celebrate effort and mathematical communication; step-by-step scaffolding |
| Growth Mindset | Stickers to celebrate mistakes and resilience; stickers to celebrate effort |
| Relevance | Meaningful contextual problems in every lesson; Enrichment Goals including Financial Literacy and Data Science |

### Personality of a MathFit Child (6 Traits)

1. **Curious about the "Why"** — doesn't just accept formulas; wants to understand reasoning behind them
2. **Embraces productive struggle** — leans into challenges; "This is where I get stronger," not "I can't do this"
3. **Explains their thinking** — can articulate *why* they think it; naturally teaches others
4. **Practices mathematical thinking daily** — puzzles, mental math, logical reasoning as naturally as brushing teeth
5. **Approaches problems systematically** — breaks down any challenge logically; asks "what do I know?" and "what do I need to find out?"
6. **Sees patterns everywhere** — spots geometric patterns in architecture, number sequences in everyday objects, mathematical beauty in unexpected places

> "MathFit isn't about being naturally gifted or becoming a mathematician. MathFit is about the ability to think clearly, reason logically, and approach life's challenges with systematic confidence. When a child is MathFit, mathematics becomes their superpower for navigating the world."
