## Cuemath vs. Key Alternatives

| Competitor         | Category                           | Where Cuemath Wins                                                                                      | Where Competitor Is Used                                  |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Khan Academy**   | Free self-paced video + practice   | Live human guidance; accountability; tutor relationship; faster gap closure                             | Free supplemental practice; low-income access             |
| **IXL**            | Adaptive practice platform         | Live instruction; explanation of "why"; tutor relationship                                              | High-volume drill practice; school supplement             |
| **Kumon**          | In-person worksheet-based tutoring | Online convenience; live interaction; concept-first vs. repetition-first; no commute                    | Parents who want in-person; worksheet discipline model    |
| **Varsity Tutors** | General tutoring marketplace       | Math-only specialization; proprietary platform; consistent tutor (not a marketplace); MathFit framework | Subject breadth (non-math tutoring needs)                 |
| **Mathnasium**     | In-person math learning center     | Online access; 1:1 vs. small group; proprietary tools; global availability                              | Local center preference; group environment motivation     |
| **Wyzant**         | Tutor marketplace                  | Curated top 1% vs. open marketplace; platform purpose-built for math; structured curriculum             | Finding specialized niche tutors; one-off sessions        |
| **Outschool**      | Online group classes               | 1:1 attention; consistent tutor; math-specific depth                                                    | Social learning; interest-based courses; younger children |

## What Cuemath Does NOT Claim to Be

- Not a homework help tool (though it helps with homework)
- Not the cheapest option (one standard price; no discount positioning)
- Not a one-size-fits-all platform — explicitly personalized per child
