# Pricing & Business

All plans: 2 classes/week, 1:1 live sessions

| Plan      | Price per class | Total classes |
| --------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| 3 Month   | $25/class       | 26 classes    |
| 6 Months  | $22.5/class     | 52 classes    |
| 12 Months | $20/class       | 104 classes   |

**Included with all plans:** Flexible Leaves, Adjustable Schedule, Easy Refunds

## Pricing Line for Blogs

Whenever Cuemath pricing needs to be mentioned in any blog, use the per-class rate with plan context:
- **K–7:** **"starting at $25/class (3-month plan)"**
- **Grades 8–12:** **"starting at $32/class (3-month plan)"**
- **Generic (when grade is unspecified):** **"starting at $25/class"**

Do not use "$200/month" — the minimum plan is now 3 months ($650 for K–7, $832 for Grades 8–12).
Do not quote total billing amounts in blog copy unless the blog is specifically about Cuemath pricing.
