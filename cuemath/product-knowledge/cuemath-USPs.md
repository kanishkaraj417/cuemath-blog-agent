### What to say about the assessment
Never call it "MathFit Evaluation" without first saying what it does for the child. The right framing is:

> "Before the first session, your child takes a short assessment that finds exactly where their understanding breaks down — not by grade level, but by concept."

The parent now understands: this is not a placement test that slots them into a level, it's a diagnostic that finds the specific concept gap. That is the value. The brand name is secondary.


### What NOT to do
- Do not open with "Cuemath is a 1:1 live online math tutoring program." (too feature-first)
- Do not name "MathFit Evaluation" as the lead point — parents will skim past a brand name they don't recognize
- Do not say "certified expert tutors" as the first signal of quality — parents want to know what happens to their child, not what credentials the tutor has
- Do not keep the "What we found" section to 1–2 sentences — it should run 3–4 paragraphs covering the full arc above
