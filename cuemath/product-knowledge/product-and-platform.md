# Product & Platform

---

## 1. Core Product

**What it is:** 1-on-1 live online math tutoring for K-12 students.
**Target audience:** K-12 students and their parents, primarily in the US.
**Format:** Live, online, one-on-one sessions with a dedicated math tutor.
**Session frequency:** 2 sessions/week
**Session duration:** 55–60 minutes per session
**Curriculum:** Aligned to US Common Core State Standards (CCSS).

### Key Differentiators

- 1-on-1 live sessions (not recorded video or AI-only)
- Math-only focus (not a general tutoring platform)
- Proprietary teaching tools (Cueboard, Talk-o-Meter, LEAP Platform, MathFit Assessments)
- Student progress tracked and reported to parents
- Tutors are credentialed and reviewed by Cuemath

---

## 2. Platform Features

Features available inside the Cuemath live tutoring platform:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Cueboard** | Interactive digital whiteboard used in live 1:1 sessions |
| **Talk-o-Meter** | Tracks student participation and engagement during class |
| **LEAP Platform** | Adaptive learning platform that adjusts to student pace |
| **MathFit Evaluation** | Pre-assessment that identifies whether a student has conceptual gaps or needs more skill-building; included free with the trial |

## 3. Cuemath App

**What it is:** Cuemath's math practice app.
### Three Sections

#### 1. Daily Workout
Five personalized exercises in every session:

| Domain | Focus |
|---|---|
| Memory | Essential math facts and formulas |
| Fluency | Mental math speed and accuracy |
| Understanding | Deep conceptual clarity |
| Application | Real-world problem-solving |
| Reasoning | Logic puzzles and pattern recognition |

#### 2. Arena
Multiplayer competitive challenges in three modes:

| Mode | Format |
|---|---|
| **Strategy Games** | 1v1 math battles |
| **Logic Puzzles** | Interactive brain-teasers |
| **Speed Rush** | Fast-paced survival challenges |

#### 3. Practice
On-demand focused practice to master specific skills.
